The limit

You changed what you shared.
But can you change what already happened?

Your revision changes what you would send next time. It doesn’t reach what you sent the first time. Here is what you can actually control, in this prototype and in a real AI service.

In this prototype

    This is a real action here. Afterwards, the comparison can no longer show your first answers.

    In a real AI service · not observable in this prototype

    • Deleting a chat usually removes it from your view. Whether and when copies are deleted from the provider’s systems depends on its retention policy.
    • Backups, logs and safety review may keep data for set periods, depending on the provider.
    • Whether conversations may be used to improve models often depends on your settings and account type, and on the provider’s policy at the time.
    • Information already counted in aggregate statistics or product analytics usually can’t be pulled back out one answer at a time.
    • Many privacy laws give you rights to access or delete your data. Using them means asking the provider, which then has to act.

    None of this is about a specific company. It describes where control sits: with the provider, not in the chat box.

        ELSEWHERE is a design research prototype, not an audit of any AI provider. Inferences are examples of what a service could guess from answers like these, not conclusions about you. Uses are general categories found in published privacy policies and vary by provider, product and settings. Nothing here was used to train a model, sold, or turned into a profile.
