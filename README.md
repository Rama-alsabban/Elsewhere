# ELSEWHERE

**An experiment in AI travel planning.**

ELSEWHERE is an interactive critical-design website about the privacy paradox in AI. It looks like a friendly AI travel planner: you pick a destination, answer up to seven optional questions, and receive a personalised itinerary and a downloadable postcard. Then the page turns around and asks what the exchange really was.

Made by Rama Alsabban for the Master's in Applied AI for Arts & Design.

## The experience

| Act | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 00 · Declaration | An optional question: how much do you care about keeping your personal information private when using AI? |
| 01 · Attraction | Seven optional questions (dates, interests, companions, where you stay, budget, preferences, schedule). Every one can be skipped. |
| 02 · Reward | Your itinerary and a postcard to download. No warnings yet. |
| 03 · Reveal | *Your trip ended. This didn't.* Three layers: **What You Receive**, **What You Provide**, **Beyond Your View**, including an interactive trace of one of your answers up to the boundary of what the prototype can verify. |
| 04 · Challenge | *Can you keep the trip without giving away as much?* Remove, generalise or replace answers and see exactly which stops change, and why. |
| 05 · Limit | *You changed what you shared. But can you change what already happened?* |
| 06 · Reflection | Your original and revised exchanges side by side, set against what you said at the start. |

## What it does and doesn't do

- Everything runs in your browser. Answers stay on the page and are gone when you close it. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
- Itineraries come from a transparent, rule-based planner and a built-in guide of real places for 23 cities. They aren't generated by an external AI service.
- Sensitive examples (an exact address, a calendar) are clearly made up.
- Inferences and uses in Layer 03 are shown as **possibilities, not events**. The prototype does not monitor any AI provider and does not claim that profiling, training, selling or any economic value took place.
- When run inside Claude, the planner can optionally look up real places for other cities. Only the place name is sent, and only with the visitor's consent. On this site, the built-in guide is always used.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repository. No build step or dependencies are needed (fonts load from Google Fonts).
